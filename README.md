### Story

I got into software because I like the moment when a messy problem starts to make sense: the data lines up, the interface clicks, and a small idea turns into something people can actually use.

These days I spend most of my time building full-stack JavaScript and TypeScript applications. I care about clean user flows, readable code, and shipping projects that feel finished instead of just technically complete. I am happiest in the middle of product thinking and engineering detail, where a feature has to be useful, maintainable, and pleasant to use.

Outside of code, I am usually following movies, music, sports, or a good anime arc. Those interests tend to find their way into my projects, from trailer discovery tools to game-day pick'em apps.

### Building Now

- [Baker World Cup Picks](https://bakerpickems.com/) - a TypeScript pick'em app for following World Cup matchups with friends.
- [Portfolio Website](https://www.joshuabaker.dev/) - my home base for projects, experience, and experiments.
